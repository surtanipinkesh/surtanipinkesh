Questions, answered

How much of my video is actually AI?

Every video uses AI somewhere (visuals, voice, motion or all three), but every frame is still directed, edited and graded by hand.

How fast is turnaround?

Social videos: 3–5 days. Commercials: 10–14 days. Rush delivery is available as an add-on.

What if I don't like the direction?

Every package includes revision rounds, and we lock a script or storyboard before any rendering starts.
