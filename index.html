01 — ABOUT

Eleven years behind the timeline.
Now editing alongside AI.

Papad Pixels is the studio of Pinkesh Surtani — a video editor and DaVinci Resolve craftsman who has spent 11+ years cutting films, commercials and brand content. Today that craft runs on a hybrid pipeline: traditional editing discipline, powered by generative AI for ideation, VFX, and commercial production — so every project moves faster without losing the human eye for story.

From full narrative edits to AI-generated commercials built frame-by-frame with next-gen tools, Papad Pixels exists to help brands and filmmakers turn raw footage — and raw ideas — into content that actually gets watched.

05 — THE EDGE

Why go AI-centric?

AI doesn't replace the edit — it removes the friction around it. Papad Pixels folds generative and assistive AI into a traditional post-production pipeline, so ideas move from brief to screen in a fraction of the time, without cutting corners on craft.
