WHAT YOU GET

What every AI commercial includes

The same deliverables a traditional production would hand over, without the shoot. You get a finished AI TV commercial plus the cutdowns you need to run it as paid social ads.

  • 16:9 horizontal + 9:16 vertical, both delivered in 4K
  • Custom character, wardrobe and environment reference sheets for your brand
  • Cinematic AI cinematography: camera moves, lighting and color grade
  • AI voiceover or licensed music, sound design and final mix
  • Text supers and finishing composited in DaVinci Resolve
  • Hook variant included, ready for paid ad testing
  • 2 revision rounds + money-back guarantee

Seedance 2.5 commercials and TVC ads

Seedance 2.5 is at the core of the pipeline, alongside Nano Banana Pro, Seedream and Higgsfield. Instead of stitching random generations together, every shot is planned on a storyboard and generated against the same reference sheets, so your product, characters and world stay consistent from the first frame to the last.

The footage is then cut, graded and mixed like a traditional TVC. The result is a 15, 30 or 60-second AI TV commercial ready for broadcast, YouTube, connected TV and Meta or TikTok placements.

AI ads for brands and businesses

AI video ads suit fashion, beauty and fragrance brands launching a product, food and beverage brands that need a hero spot, ecommerce businesses testing new ad creative, and agencies that need extra AI production capacity.

Because nothing has to be filmed, you can explore bigger ideas (a desert at golden hour, a runway on the moon, a cow with opinions about cheese) at a fraction of the cost of a location shoot.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is an AI video ad?

An AI video ad is a commercial whose footage is generated with AI video models instead of being filmed. At Papad Pixels the AI generation is directed shot by shot and then edited, graded and mixed by hand, so the result looks and sounds like a finished TV commercial.

How much does an AI TV commercial cost?

AI Commercials start at $595 for 15 seconds, $995 for 30 seconds and $1,595 for 60 seconds. Bigger productions, like multi-scene brand films, are quoted as a custom package.

Which AI tools do you use?

Seedance 2.5 is the main video model, alongside Nano Banana Pro, Seedream and Higgsfield. Editing, grading and finishing happen in DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro.

Can you keep my product and characters consistent?

Yes. Every project starts with custom reference sheets for your product, characters, wardrobe and environments, and every shot is generated against them.

Do you make AI commercials for perfume, fashion and food brands?

Yes. Most of the portfolio is fragrance, beauty, fashion and food work, from luxury perfume films to ice cream and cheese commercials. The same process works for any physical product: reference sheets are built for it and every shot is generated around it.

Do you work with brands in the USA, Italy and Spain?

Yes. Papad Pixels works remotely with brands worldwide, including the USA, Italy and Spain. Briefs, reviews and delivery all happen online, and multi-language voiceover versions are available.
