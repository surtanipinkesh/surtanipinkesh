  • AI voiceover matched to your audience

Why brands use AI UGC ads

UGC-style ads work because they look like a real person recommending a product. Making them with AI means you can test many hooks, angles and presenters quickly, keep the same spokesperson across a whole campaign, and skip shipping products to creators.

It works especially well for beauty, fragrance, fashion, food and app brands that need a steady stream of fresh creative for Meta, TikTok and YouTube ads.

AI UGC in other languages

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is AI UGC?

AI UGC is user-generated-content style video, like a creator demoing or recommending a product, made with AI instead of a filmed creator. It keeps the authentic feel of UGC ads while making it fast to produce many variations.

Which platforms are AI UGC videos made for?

TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Meta ads first, delivered in 9:16 vertical. 16:9 and 1:1 versions are available on request.

Can you make versions in other languages?

Yes. Voiceover and captions can be delivered in other languages, so one AI UGC concept can run in several markets.

How much does an AI UGC video cost?

AI UGC videos are priced per video, depending on length and how many variations you need. Send a brief through the contact form for a quote.
